# rwx_drafts — rw-exploit candidates (2026-08-04)

Authored variants of the three backbones that beat richwoman (stock
foul-play), each pushing a DIFFERENT one of the measured winning elements.
Not clones: mons and sets may repeat across the family, but each team's
wincon structure is its own.

- **rwx1_primarina_dualcm** (18_primarina backbone): dual bulky-CM sustain
  wincons (Primarina CM+Draining Kiss, Raging Bolt bulky CM) she cannot
  chip out, plus the hazard reset 18 never had (Court Change Cinderace) and
  a full anti-snowball kit (Haze, WoW, Dragon Tail, trapper Taunt Heatran).
- **rwx2_hydrapple_wish** (27_hydrapple backbone): the triple-sustain
  variant — NP+Regenerator Hydrapple and ID+Body Press Zamazenta stay, and
  Slowking-Galar gives way to Wish Alomomola, patching Zamazenta's
  no-recovery hole that long chip wars expose. Moltres trades Hurricane for
  Will-O-Wisp (Roar stays). SD+spin Excadrill is the hazard answer a
  Gholdengo cannot afford to block.
- **rwx3_cinderace_triwin** (ah3 backbone): wincon density + Unaware —
  Corviknight becomes a third setup wincon (ID+Body Press, Defog dropped;
  Court Change owns hazards), Toxapex gives way to Unaware Clodsire (hard
  stop to her CM/NP snowballs, Haze on top). Five Heavy-Duty Boots.
