dittoab3_0806 — is it DITTO, or is it the SLOT? PREREGISTERED 2026-08-06 ~17:05
================================================================================

WHY. dittoab0806 measured the Ditto slot at +12.67pp [+9.11, +16.17] vs stock
fp — real, artifact-free, control arm sitting in our historical band. But the
PREDICTED cause was refuted in the same read: the gain is anti-correlated with
opponent setup rate (low-setup half +17.1pp, high-setup half +8.2pp), so
"revenge-kills boosted sweepers that fp cannot price" is NOT what is winning.

The leading alternative was engine-pilot fit: Ditto replaced Salamence (a
Dragon Dance SETUP SWEEPER, the class [[project_monotype_engine_pilot_bias]]
says our engine underuses). USER OBJECTION, 2026-08-06, and it is decisive
against that story: the OTHER two replaced mons are not setup sweepers at all
— ct2's Meowscarada is a CHOICE SCARF revenge killer (Trick/U-turn/Knock
Off/Triple Axel), i.e. Ditto's own role, and ct3's Latios is a Choice Scarf
attacker. If pilot-fit-with-setup-sweepers drove the result, dt1 (Salamence)
would dominate. Measured ordering is the reverse: dt2 +17.4 > dt1 +15.8 >>
dt3 +4.8. The biggest gain came from a LIKE-FOR-LIKE revenge-killer swap.

QUESTION. Is the +12.67pp a property of DITTO, or of the SLOT — i.e. would any
competent engine-friendly attacker in that slot beat the mons it replaced?

ARMS. Three arms x three teams, one slot different, nine teams total:
  ct{1,2,3}  original pool teams (Salamence / Scarf Meowscarada / Scarf Latios)
  dt{1,2,3}  Scarf Imposter Ditto            (the dittoab0806 arm, re-run)
  bb{1,2,3}  Choice Band Dragonite           (Multiscale, Outrage/ESpeed/EQ/
             Fire Punch — no setup move, self-sufficient, priority; the
             "engine-pilotable breaker" archetype)
All nine validate clean vs the gen9ou proxy.

DESIGN. par_series dittoab3_0806, N=4500, 12 lanes, --suite ditto_ab3
--fp-suite rw_proxy, stock knobs, 300ms. 9 x 12 = 108 pairings, ~42 games
each. All three arms run in ONE queue so they are interleaved in time and
share opponents — dt and ct are deliberately RE-RUN rather than compared
across runs, which both gives a same-run three-way comparison and replicates
the +12.67pp (cross-run level steps have burned this campaign three times).

POWER: 1500/arm -> the dt-vs-bb and bb-vs-ct contrasts each resolve ~5.1pp.

DECISION RULE (before launch):

  - dt-vs-ct must also REPLICATE within [+9.1, +16.2] or the original run is

REPLICATION PASSED: dt-ct point estimate +10.47 sits inside the preregistered
falsifier band [+9.1, +16.2] from dittoab0806 (+12.67), CIs overlap heavily.

VERDICT PER THE RULE: bb ~= ct, both well below dt -> THE GAIN IS
DITTO-SPECIFIC. A generic competent sixth (Choice Band Dragonite) does NOT
reproduce it; these teams did not merely have a weak slot.

TWO HYPOTHESES DIED IN THE SAME READ:
1. "Archetype-agnostic mirror" (that Ditto is uniquely opponent-independent)
   predicted dt flat while ct/bb swing across the setup split. Measured swing
   low->high setup: ct +7.7pp, bb +8.8pp, dt +8.2pp — ALL THE SAME. Ditto is
   a roughly CONSTANT ~+10pp level shift, not a matchup patch. This also
   means the dittoab0806 pattern I built that hypothesis on (dt flat, ct
   collapsing) DID NOT REPLICATE — it was cell noise at n=750.
2. "Revenge-kills boosted sweepers" is not the driver either, now on
   positive evidence rather than the (retracted) anti-correlation: copies
   onto a CLEAN target convert at 40.7% vs 53.6% for boosted ones, and
   because 69% of copies are clean, **63% of all Ditto revenge kills come
   from copying an UNBOOSTED mon**. The boost case was always the minority.

WHAT SURVIVES as the mechanism statement: Scarf Imposter is a UNIVERSAL
revenge killer — it copies whatever is in front of it and, holding the Scarf
the target does not, outspeeds it whether or not it has boosted. That is
Imposter-specific (a Band breaker has fixed typing and coverage; Ditto always
matches), matchup-general (hence the constant level shift and zero setup
interaction), and it predicts the 2.6x boost-seeking enrichment is a
SIDE-EFFECT of the eval's large boost credit rather than the source of value.
NEXT TEST (not run): eval A/B with the boosts term muted (eval_off knob). If
Ditto's edge survives or grows, the boost credit is bait and the engine is
leaving value on the table by hunting stages.
