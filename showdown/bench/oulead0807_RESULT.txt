ou_lead_net — OPPONENT-LEAD PREDICTION NET, GATE FAILED 2026-08-07
================================================================================
BUILT. showdown/extract_ou_lead_data.py (new) + monotype/train_lead_net.py
(reused unchanged) -> showdown/ou_lead_net.pt.
Corpus: 6,574 of 7,508 gen9ou replays (13,148 examples by symmetry, vs the
monotype net's 5,244). Retention needed a CHAOS FALLBACK: the curated PS index
covers 118 species but replays contain the whole tier, so requiring a curated
set for all 12 mons dropped 75% of replays (51 distinct missing species in a
60-replay probe). Chaos covers 408 species and took retention to ~88%.
TRAINING: 39.5% val top-1, 77.1% top-3, vs 23.9% majority-class and 16.7%
random. The net clearly learned the human OU lead meta.

GATE (the point of the exercise): beat the scouting book, which is what
`_lead_ev_blend` actually uses. Scored on 884 real ladder games vs booked
opponents:
    net           20.2%
    book          32.1%   (in-sample — the book is BUILT from these logs)
    field prior   35.6%   (also in-sample)
  leave-one-out, baselines made out-of-sample like the net:
    book          29.3%
    field prior   29.9%
    net           20.2%
FAILS EVEN AFTER THE FAIRNESS CORRECTION, and by ~9pp. Per opponent the net
lost to the book on every one: richwoman 27.7 vs 41.2, gem31fl 11.2 vs 21.8,
gem3f 17.0 vs 21.6, gem31p 9.2 vs 28.9.

DIAGNOSIS — TRAINING/DEPLOYMENT DISTRIBUTION MISMATCH, and it is the useful
finding here. The net scores 39.5% on held-out HUMAN replays and 20.2% on our
actual opponents, barely above the 16.7% random floor. It learned how HUMANS
lead in gen9 OU. Our ladder opponents are richwoman (foul-play) and three LLM
bots, and they do not lead like humans.
CONSEQUENCE FOR OCTOBER, which is the part that generalises: the PokeAgent
field is AI agents, not humans. So human replay data is the WRONG training
distribution for predicting entrants' leads, and this result says so at 884
games rather than after the competition. Any future opponent-behaviour model
should be trained on AGENT behaviour — our own ladder logs and the archive of
bot games — not on Smogon replays, however plentiful those are.
ALSO NOTE the simple opponent-AGNOSTIC field prior (29.9% LOO) is as good as
the per-opponent book (29.3%): at these sample sizes, knowing WHICH bot you
face adds nothing over knowing what bots lead in general.

KEEP: the extractor, the corpus and the net are all reusable the moment an
agent-behaviour corpus exists — the pipeline is the asset, the weights are not.
DO NOT wire ou_lead_net.pt into _lead_ev_blend.
