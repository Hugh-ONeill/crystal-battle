THE NULL IS UNINFORMATIVE, AND THAT WAS PREDICTABLE FROM THE DESIGN — do not
file it as "the lever does not convert". If the measured deltas are causal,
the behavioural shift implies only +2.82pp overall:
  glimmora +26.9pp shift x +12.5 = +1.12 | samurotthisui +0.75 | cinderace
  +0.47 | kingambit +0.26 | ceruledge +0.22  (each family is 1/3 of games)
This run resolves 6.26pp at 80% power (n=1000/arm). Resolving 2.82pp needs
~4,900 games/ARM, ~9,850 total (~8h at 20 games/min). So the winrate arm of
this run never had the power to see its own prediction, and the honest status
is UNTESTED, not refuted.
LESSON WORTH KEEPING: a lever that only fires on a fraction of games has its
effect diluted by that fraction. KG leads are 8.7% of the third of games that
CAN lead it = 2.9% of all games, so even perfectly eliminating a -8.9pp
mistake buys 0.26pp overall. Compute the DILUTED effect before sizing any
behavioural lever's winrate test.

T1-REPUDIATION CHECK (user question, 2026-08-07 ~11:30): "of the turns they
are being nudged to lead, are they just switching out?" — PARTLY YES.
Turn-1 switch rate by lead species, arm A -> arm B, and the MARGINAL rate on
the leads the prior added:
  glimmora       10.9% -> 22.7%   90 extra leads, MARGINAL T1-switch 31.1%
  samurotthisui   9.5% -> 21.6%   74 extra leads, MARGINAL 33.8%
  cinderace      58.7% -> 63.0%   83 extra leads, MARGINAL 66.3%
Overall T1-switch 20.6% -> 23.9%.
So roughly a THIRD of the nudged glimmora/samurott leads and TWO THIRDS of the
nudged cinderace leads are immediately repudiated — nominal leads, not real
ones. Discounting them, the +0.78pp nominal gain from these three species
becomes ~+0.48pp effective, and that discount is OPTIMISTIC because each
repudiation also hands over a turn.
This is the near-tie sampler's known signature reappearing: rank-1 leads hold
at ~14% T1-switch while forced/sampled lower-ranked leads get repudiated at
24-25% (CB_LEAD_ARGMAX attribution, 2026-08-04). Overriding the matrix
manufactures T1 switches, which is the very gap the campaign has been trying
to close ([[project_gen9_behavior_gap]]).

SEPARATE AND ARGUABLY BIGGER FINDING — A PRE-EXISTING PATHOLOGY, NOT CAUSED
BY THE PRIOR. In the STOCK arm several leads are already mostly nominal:
  cinderace 58.7%, scizor 59.6%, ragingbolt 44.7% turn-1 switch.
The preview search is choosing leads it then immediately abandons, in the
default configuration, on ~1 in 5 games overall. Cinderace has no pivot move
(HJK/Pyro Ball/Court Change/Sucker Punch), so these are genuine repudiations
rather than U-turn tempo. Worth its own investigation: either the preview
matrix and the in-battle search disagree systematically, or the lead is being
chosen for a matchup the first turn immediately disproves.
