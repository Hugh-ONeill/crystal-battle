boostscale0806 — is the eval's stat-stage credit OVERSIZED? PREREGISTERED 2026-08-06 ~22:25
================================================================================

WHY. Measured today on a real state: the eval pays +26.9 for a copied +2 Atk,
+30.0 for +1 Spe, +43.5 for a full Dragon Dance, +86.9 for +2/+2 — against
POKEMON_ALIVE = +30 for an entire extra Pokemon. In the Ditto screen the
search entered on a boosted target 2.6x more often than the base rate (31.1%
of transforms vs 12.2% of available turns), yet 63% of Ditto's actual revenge
kills came from copying an UNBOOSTED mon, and boosted copies convert WORSE
against the opponents that offer the most of them (50.5% vs 61.5%). That is
the signature of a credit the search chases harder than it pays.
Mechanically the pull is structural: a copied stage is banked at the LEAF
immediately, while a better answer usually has to be played out over several
plies — at a 300ms budget an instant reward outcompetes an earned one.

KNOB (new, engine commit this session). CB_BOOST_SCALE multiplies every stat
stage credit on BOTH sides, so the eval stays unbiased between them; default
1.0, unset = unchanged. Deliberately a SCALE, not an on/off: zeroing it would
also blind the engine to the OPPONENT's boosts, which is a different and
probably worse change. This tests MAGNITUDE only. Pinned by an engine test
that the default leaves the credit live (950 engine tests green, 899 python).

ARMS. par_series boostscale0806, N=2000, 12 lanes, --suite ditto_ab
--fp-suite rw_proxy, --ab "CB_BOOST_SCALE=0.5".
  arm A = stock eval (scale 1.0)      arm B = half-weight stages (0.5)
Same-team game pairs, two persistent workers per lane (CB_* knobs are
OnceLock-per-process, so process isolation IS config isolation). Suite is the
SIX-team ditto_ab set on purpose: pooled discordant pairs answer the general
eval question at full power, and the dt/ct split is a secondary read of
whether any effect is Ditto-specific.

PRIMARY. Sign test on DISCORDANT PAIRS over the dispense-order pair prefix
(paired_tally.py). 0.5 = no difference. Run-scoped drift cancels by
construction. ~1000 pairs (~400 discordant expected); the rig's stated sensitivity is
~8pp of discordant share at ~89 pairs, so this is comfortably powered.

DECISION RULE (before launch):
  - arm B better, excluding 0.5 -> the stage credit IS oversized; follow up
    with a scale sweep (0.25/0.5/0.75) to site the optimum before changing
    the default. Do NOT ship 0.5 off one A/B.
  - arm A better, excluding 0.5 -> the credit is correctly sized or too
    small; the boost-chasing is good play and the Ditto mechanism question
    stands unresolved on this axis.
  - spans 0.5 -> report as an upper bound on the miscalibration; the credit
    is not costing measurable winrate at this scale factor, and the 2.6x
    enrichment is behaviour without consequence.
  - The dt/ct split NEVER overrides the pooled read at this n.

NOTE this is an EVAL-CALIBRATION experiment that happens to be motivated by
Ditto — the result generalises to every team we run, which is why it is worth
more than another Ditto-specific run.
