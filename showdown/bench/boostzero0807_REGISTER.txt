boostzero0807 — is the stat-stage eval term INERT? PREREGISTERED 2026-08-07 ~07:00
================================================================================

WHY. boostscale0806 halved the stage credit and moved NOTHING: winrate null
(discordant share 50.1% [45.4, 54.8]) AND our own setup rate unchanged (7.00%
vs 6.90% at >21k move selections per arm). Both hypotheses failed — mine (the
credit is oversized and the search over-chases copies) and the user's (the
credit is load-bearing, it is the eval's only channel for valuing a setup move
at the leaf, which is why RL agents famously will not set up).

The explanation that fits both nulls: the term is REDUNDANT. mcts.rs `rollout`
does NO playout — it is a single `evaluate(state)` sigmoided against the root
— so the eval is the only value source in the search. BUT the eval reads HP
FRACTION, and the engine's damage_calc converts a stat advantage into HP loss
a few plies deeper. A +2 attacker therefore looks good WITHOUT any stage
credit: it simply punches harder in the simulated branches and the HP term
scores that. The stage credit is a shortcut for something the search already
computes properly, which is exactly why scaling it does nothing.

TEST. Go to the extreme rather than half measures, and read BEHAVIOUR rather
than winrate — behaviour is ~20x denser (one observation per move selection
vs one bit per game), so a small run has large power.
par_series boostzero0807, N=1000, 12 lanes, --suite ditto_drafts
--fp-suite rw_proxy, --ab "CB_BOOST_SCALE=0".
  arm A = stock (scale 1.0)      arm B = stage credit ENTIRELY OFF
Suite is the three Ditto drafts on purpose: they carry both the setup mons
(Ceruledge SD / Dragapult DD / Kingambit SD) and the Ditto that generates
transforms, so one run yields both reads.

PRIMARY (behavioural, per arm):
 1. OUR setup-move selection rate. Stock baseline 7.00%. At ~10,600 selections
    per arm this resolves +-0.5pp.
 2. Ditto boost-seeking ENRICHMENT: share of transforms landing on a boosted
    target. Stock baseline 31.1% against a 12.2% base rate = 2.6x. At ~970
    transforms per arm this resolves +-2.9pp.
SECONDARY: winrate/discordant pairs, reported but NOT decisive at N=1000 —
this run is not powered for it and will not be read as if it were.

DECISION RULE (before launch):
  - BOTH behavioural numbers flat with the term entirely off -> the constant
    is INERT. It can be retired (or cut hard) with no behavioural cost, and
    the boost-value channel is confirmed to be HP-via-damage_calc. Follow up
    by deleting it and re-running the standard non-regression A/B before any
    default change ships.
  - Setup rate DROPS materially -> the user's hypothesis was right after all
    and 0.5 was simply too gentle a scaling to show it; the term is
    load-bearing for setup and must be SPLIT (credit earned stages separately
    from mirrored/opposing ones), never simply cut.
  - Enrichment drops but setup rate holds -> the credit specifically drives
    Ditto's boost-hunting without driving our own setup; that is the
    cleanest possible case for splitting the term by source.
  - Any behavioural move without a winrate move is still informative: it
    would mean the term steers play without steering outcomes, which is the
    campaign's most common finding and worth recording as such.

NO PEEKING at outcomes before N=1000. ETA ~51 min at the measured 19.8
games/min. Box is free (ladder stopped by the user at ~06:52).

RESULT 2026-08-07 07:49 (1000/1000, ~52 min, 12 lanes clean).
PRIMARY 1 — OUR OWN SETUP RATE: **the term IS load-bearing, decisively.**
  arm A (stock)          634/10481 = 6.05% [5.61%, 6.52%]
  arm B (credit OFF)     447/10584 = 4.22% [3.86%, 4.62%]
  Non-overlapping CIs; a 30% RELATIVE collapse in setup-move selection.
  THE USER'S HYPOTHESIS IS CONFIRMED: because evaluate_pokemon is stat-blind,
  the stage credit is the eval's only leaf-level reason to spend a turn
  setting up, and removing it makes the engine stop.
PRIMARY 2 — DITTO BOOST-SEEKING: **NOT driven by the term.**
  enrichment (share of transforms onto a boosted target / base rate)
  arm A 27.3% / 12.4% = 2.2x     arm B 31.8% / 15.1% = 2.1x  — unchanged.
  So the 2.6x boost-hunting measured on the screen is the SEARCH finding
  boosted targets through damage_calc, not the eval baiting it.
SECONDARY (underpowered by design, NOT decisive): 226 discordant pairs, arm A
share 46.5% [39.9%, 53.0%] — spans 0.5. Arms 51.0% vs 54.2%.

THE FINDING IS A DISSOCIATION. One constant is doing two unrelated jobs:
  (a) paying for OUR setup moves — load-bearing, dies at 0.0;
  (b) pricing a copied/opposing stage — inert, the rollouts already carry it
      via damage_calc (this is what the 2026-08-06 half-scale null measured,
      and why that null was NOT evidence the term is useless).
This also explains the 0.5-vs-0.0 discrepancy: the response is THRESHOLDED,
not linear. At half weight setup still cleared the bar for clicking; at zero
it does not. A single scale factor cannot separate (a) from (b) — SPLITTING
the term by source is the indicated design, exactly as the 08-06 register
predicted for this branch of the decision rule.
DO NOT cut or rescale the credit. Next: split it — credit stages WE EARNED by
spending a turn separately from stages that are merely present (copied,
inherited, or the opponent's) — then A/B the halves independently.
