CORRECTION 2026-08-06 ~17:40 — "MECHANISM REFUTED" WAS OVERSTATED.
The refutation above rested on the gain being anti-correlated with opponent
setup rate. Decomposing that gain by ARM shows it is a CONTROL-arm artifact:
                      ditto arm    control arm    gain
  low-setup foes        50.2%         33.1%      +17.1pp
  high-setup foes       53.3%         45.1%       +8.2pp
The DITTO arm is essentially flat across the split (50.2 -> 53.3, if anything
slightly BETTER against setup-heavy teams). All the variation is the control
teams collapsing to 33% against the bulky/stall/Darkrai archetypes. So the
anti-correlation says nothing about Ditto and everything about a hole in the
three base teams. HONEST STATUS: the blindness thesis is UNSUPPORTED (Ditto is
not dramatically better against setup-heavy opponents), not REFUTED. I read a
baseline weakness as a Ditto pattern; comparing a DIFFERENCE across a split
without decomposing both arms is the error, and it is the same shape as the
in-flight-numerator and wrong-log-file mistakes earlier today.

Mechanism detail that also refutes the user's mistiming hypothesis (checked on
the 720-game screen): Ditto is NOT copying the wrong mon and getting run over.
Against setup-heavy foes 45.3% of copies land on an already-boosted target (vs
17.5% against low-setup foes), so the engine finds the right target; and of the
clean-copy Ditto deaths, only 1.3% (low) / 3.4% (high) occur while the opponent
is boosted. Ditto's local stats are near-identical across halves (death rate
13-16%, kill rate 46-62%).

LEADING HYPOTHESIS NOW: Ditto is an ARCHETYPE-AGNOSTIC answer — a Scarfed
mirror of whatever is in front of it — so its own winrate is flat (~50-53%)
regardless of opponent style, and the measured gain is largest exactly where
the base team is weakest. Testable: it predicts the gain tracks CONTROL-ARM
WEAKNESS per matchup, not any property of Ditto. The dittoab3 Band-breaker arm
tests the complementary question (Ditto vs any competent sixth).
