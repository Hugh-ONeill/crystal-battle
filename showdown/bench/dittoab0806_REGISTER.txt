dittoab0806 — does the Ditto slot PAY? PREREGISTERED 2026-08-06 ~13:30
================================================================================

QUESTION. dtscreen0806 established the BENEFIT side only: Ditto deploys (1.94
copies/game, 96% of games), 31% of copies land on a boosted target, 53.6% of
those kill the target — about 0.32 revenge kills per game — and foul-play is
provably blind to the threat (setup rate 7.81% with a live Ditto benched vs
8.08% without: indistinguishable). It could NOT price the COST: the slot Ditto
takes from a proven team. Net value is benefit minus cost, and only this run
measures the difference.

ARMS. Six teams, three matched pairs, differing in ONE slot each:
  dt1_glimmora_ho      vs  ct1_glimmora_ho_orig       (Ditto <-> Salamence)
  dt2_tinglu_balance   vs  ct2_tinglu_balance_orig    (Ditto <-> Scarf Meowscarada)
  dt3_lando_bulky      vs  ct3_lando_bulky_orig       (Ditto <-> Scarf Latios)
Controls are the unmodified pool_hl teams (ho2/33/36). All six validate clean.

DESIGN. par_series dittoab0806, N=3000, 12 lanes, --suite ditto_ab --fp-suite
rw_proxy, stock knobs, 300ms both sides. NOT --ab: that knob varies an ENV
VAR on one team, and the variable here is the team itself. Instead the queue's
index rotation enumerates 6 our-teams x 12 fp-teams = 72 pairings, ~42 games
each, and lanes pull from ONE queue, so the arms are interleaved in time and
balanced across opponents by construction — run-scoped drift hits both arms
alternately, the property --ab exists to guarantee.

POWER (computed BEFORE launch, at p~0.5, alpha .05, 80% power):
  per pair, 500/arm    -> resolves 8.9pp
  pooled,  1500/arm    -> resolves 5.1pp
So the POOLED difference is the primary estimand; per-pair rows are secondary
and underpowered for anything below ~9pp. A null here means "no effect larger
than ~5pp", NOT "no effect" — Ditto's measured benefit is ~0.32 kills/game and
a slot is worth several points, so a true effect could plausibly sit under the
resolution. Say so in the verdict rather than claiming parity.

PRIMARY. Pooled winrate, ditto arm minus control arm, with a 95% CI on the
difference. Secondary: the three per-pair rows; mechanism reads re-run on the
ditto arm as a consistency check against the screen.

DECISION RULE (written before launch):
  - CI excludes 0 in favour of Ditto -> the slot pays; keep all three on the
    ladder and draft a fourth around the same idea.
  - CI excludes 0 against -> the blindness does NOT offset the cost; pull the
    drafts from pool_hl, keep the engine/translator work (it is correctness,
    not strategy).
  - CI spans 0 -> report the interval honestly as an upper bound on the
    effect; keep the drafts on the ladder trial (the October field, not fp, is
    the target and richwoman/LLM-bots are different opponents), and do NOT
    re-run for significance — this is exactly the sequential-peeking trap.
  - Per-pair rows never override the pooled read at this n.

LANE COUNT 12, chosen BEFORE launch and before any data (amended from 8 in
the same sitting). Ceiling reasoning, since this is the constraint that
actually binds: the search budget is WALL-CLOCK (300ms/decision both sides),
so saturating the CPU does not merely slow the run, it buys fewer visits per
decision and makes both engines play worse. Measured draw at 8 lanes was 12.8
of 24 cores = 1.6 cores/lane, so saturation arrives around 15 lanes; 12 lanes
~= 19 cores leaves real headroom and matches the recorded single-arm cap.
RAM is not a factor at 60G (8 lanes used 3.8G of a 24G cap). Note the
interleaved design means CPU pressure would hit both arms alternately and
cancel in the DIFFERENCE anyway — the reason to stay below saturation is
external validity (measuring Ditto at representative search depth), not bias.

NO PEEKING: no winrate is read before N=3000 lands. Lane health and crash
counts only. ETA ~2.7h at 12 lanes (done ~16:20, well clear of the 01:00
ladder).
